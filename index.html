Japan survival guide

Before you fly ✈️

  • Complete the Japan customs / immigration information ahead of time through Visit Japan Web and screenshot the QR code.
  • Fly into Haneda if possible — it is closer, although both Tokyo airports work.
  • Get an eSIM about 2 days before the trip. Suggested options include Saily and Ubigi.
  • Ryokans and onsens can sell out fast, especially around Hakone and Kawaguchiko.
  • Consider an International Driving Permit from AAA — about $20 per person.

Money & transportation 💴

  • Suica can be added to your phone: Apple Wallet → + → Transit Card → Japan → Suica.
  • Tokyo — card.
  • Kyoto — cash.
  • Osaka — card.
  • Bring your passport everywhere for tax-free shopping.
