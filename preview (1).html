Before You Go

Japan Travel Tips

Important things to take care of before and during the trip.

🛃

Japan Customs / Visit Japan Web

Complete the Japan customs form ahead of time and take a screenshot of your QR code.

📱

Suica on Your Phone

Suica can be added to Apple Wallet and used for trains and payments at participating locations.

Apple Wallet → "+" Button → Transit Card → Japan → Suica

🛍️

Tax-Free Shopping

Bring your passport with you when shopping if you plan to use tax-free shopping.

✈️

Fly Into Haneda

Haneda is closer to Tokyo and generally more convenient, although both Tokyo airports work.

📶

Get an eSIM

Recommended to purchase an eSIM approximately 8–12 days before the trip, or around 2 days before if preferred.

♨️

Ryokans & Onsen Sell Out Fast

Ryokans and onsen can sell out quickly, especially around Hakone and Kawaguchiko. Book early.

🚗

International Driving Permit

If you plan to drive, look into getting an International Driving Permit through AAA.

💴

Carry Some Cash

Even though cards are widely useful, keep Japanese yen available, especially for Kyoto.
